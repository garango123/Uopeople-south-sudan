# 🌍 UoPeople South Sudan – Student Connect

### One Community. One Table. One Learning Journey.

**Connecting University of the People students across South Sudan through collaboration, communication, academic discussions, and shared learning experiences.**

---

## 🌐 About the Project

UoPeople South Sudan – Student Connect is a digital community platform designed to bring together students of the University of the People (UoPeople) living and studying across South Sudan.

The platform provides a common virtual space where students from different academic departments, programmes, study levels, and geographical locations can connect, interact, exchange knowledge, discuss academic challenges, and support one another throughout their educational journeys.

Our vision is to create a connected student community where geographical distance does not become a barrier to academic collaboration and meaningful relationships.

The platform is designed to make every student feel represented, welcomed, and connected.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To establish an inclusive, collaborative, and supportive digital community that connects UoPeople students throughout South Sudan and promotes academic excellence, peer learning, communication, and student engagement.

## 🌟 Our Vision

To become a central digital meeting place for UoPeople students in South Sudan, fostering a united academic community where students learn together, share experiences, develop leadership skills, and contribute to one another's success.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Student Registration and Authentication
- Student account registration.
- Email and password authentication.
- Google account authentication.
- Individual student profiles.

### 2. Student Academic Profiles

Each student can create a profile containing:

- Full name.
- Academic programme.
- Department or college.
- Study level.
- Academic year or stage.
- State or region of residence/study.

Example:

**Student:** James Michael  
**University:** University of the People  
**Programme:** Bachelor's in Computer Science  
**Study Level:** Undergraduate  
**Academic Year:** Second Year  
**Location:** Central Equatoria, South Sudan

### 3. Online Community Meetings

The platform provides an online meeting environment where students can:

- Participate in virtual meetings.
- Discuss academic experiences.
- Share ideas and knowledge.
- Ask questions.
- Exchange learning strategies.
- Support fellow students.

### 4. Interactive Speaker Identification

One of the platform's unique features is its student speaker identification system.

When a student takes the opportunity to speak, their academic profile can be displayed to meeting participants.

The information includes:

- Speaker's name.
- Academic programme.
- Department.
- Study level.
- Academic year.
- Region or state.

This helps students understand who is contributing to the discussion.

### 5. Events and Announcements

Students can stay informed about:

- Upcoming community meetings.
- Academic discussions.
- Student engagement activities.
- Learning sessions.
- Community announcements.

### 6. Meeting Agendas

Meeting agendas help participants understand the purpose and direction of each meeting.

Students can review discussion topics before participating.

### 7. Inclusive Student Community

The platform welcomes students from different academic backgrounds, including:

- Computer Science.
- Business Administration.
- Health Science.
- Education.
- Other academic programmes.

It accommodates undergraduate and postgraduate students.

### 8. Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

- Desktop computers.
- Laptops.
- Android smartphones.
- iPhones.
- Tablets.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

Student privacy and responsible information management are important considerations.

The platform follows these principles:

- Students should provide only necessary profile information.
- Regional location information should be optional.
- Precise residential addresses should never be displayed.
- Authentication is required for access to community information.
- Student information should only be accessible to authorized community participants.
- Meeting recordings should not be made without appropriate consent.
- Community moderators should promote respectful participation.
